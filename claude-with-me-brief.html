Talk to Sev / Claude With Me

Before our
hour together.

A short brief so I can walk into our 60 minutes already knowing how you work, what's slow, and what you actually want Claude to take off your plate. Record your answers as Looms (screen-share where it helps) or voice memos. Talk to me like you're explaining your week to a smart friend, not writing a report.

Pre-Session

The warm-up. You and your business in plain English.

Don't overthink these. One recording, top to bottom, off the cuff. Aim for 8 to 12 minutes. We'll get into the workflow specifics in the Deep End.

  1. 01
    Introduce yourself. Your name, where you're based, what you actually do day to day.
  2. 02
    How big is the operation? Just you, a small team, contractors, something in between?
  3. 03
    Walk me through a typical week. What does Monday morning look like? When are you busiest?
  4. 04
    What part of the work do you genuinely love? What part quietly drains you?
  5. 05
    How are you feeling about AI right now? Excited, sceptical, overwhelmed, all three?
  6. 06
    What made you book this session? What were you hoping it would unlock for you?
  7. 07
    If our hour together went better than you expected, what would you walk away with?
  8. 08
    Anything I should know about you, your business, or how you work before we hit record together?
Pro tip One messy recording beats five polished ones. The pauses, the asides, the "actually, wait, let me back up" moments are exactly where the real intel lives. Don't restart. Just keep going.
Going Deeper

The specifics that turn our hour into a clear plan of attack.

This is the meat. Where the boring repetitive stuff lives, what tools you actually use, what you've tried with AI, and the one thing you want working by the time we hang up. Tackle it as one long Loom, or one per category. Whatever flows.

A / Your Workflow

The repetitive stuff that eats your week.

  1. 01
    Loom me through the 3 things you do most often in a week. Share your screen and just narrate what you click, where you go, what you copy and paste.
  2. 02
    What's the most repetitive task in your week? The one you've done 50+ times already this year.
  3. 03
    What's the task you dread most? Is it boring, fiddly, easy to mess up, or just lonely?
  4. 04
    Where does most of your time go that doesn't feel like real work? (Inbox, admin, chasing, formatting, switching tabs?)
  5. 05
    If a smart assistant could quietly do one thing for you starting Monday, what would it be?
B / Your Stack

What you use and where your stuff lives.

  1. 06
    List every tool you log into in a week. Even the small ones. Even the ones you're slightly embarrassed about.
  2. 07
    Where do your notes, ideas, and admin actually live? Notion, Apple Notes, Google Docs, paper, your head?
  3. 08
    Where does your customer or client data live? CRM, spreadsheet, inbox, memory, all of the above?
  4. 09
    What's one tool you pay for and barely use? And one you wish existed but doesn't?
C / AI Today

What you've tried, what's stuck, what's worried you.

  1. 10
    Have you used Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or any other AI tool? Walk me through what you've used it for and how often.
  2. 11
    What's worked? And what's flopped or felt like a complete waste of time?
  3. 12
    What's something you've heard AI can do that you don't quite believe yet?
  4. 13
    What's the worry or hesitation you have about leaning more on AI in your business? Privacy, trust, accuracy, your team, your craft?
D / The Target

What we're actually solving in our hour.

  1. 14
    What's the ONE thing you'd love to have working by the time we hang up? Be specific.
  2. 15
    If we only had 15 minutes together, what would you spend it on?
  3. 16
    What would make this session a complete waste of time for you? So I know exactly what to avoid.
The bonus take Spend 5 minutes Looming your screen while you do a real task you'd have done today anyway. Don't prep, don't tidy, don't explain it well. Just narrate what you're clicking and why. That single raw recording is worth more than any answer you script.
The How

Loom is the hero. Voice memo if you're walking the dog.

Loom lets you screen-share while you talk, which is gold for the workflow questions. It's free, takes 2 minutes to install, and gives you a shareable link instantly. Voice memos are perfectly fine for the reflective stuff.

Recommended / Desktop

Loom on Mac or Windows

Free. Screen + face + audio. Generates a shareable link the moment you stop recording.
  1. Go to loom.com/download and grab the desktop app. Sign up with email or Google (free plan is plenty).
  2. Open Loom. Pick Screen + Cam for the workflow walkthroughs, or Cam Only for the reflective questions.
  3. Hit Cmd + Shift + L (Mac) or Ctrl + Shift + L (Windows) to start. Or click the Loom icon in your menu bar.
  4. Talk. Click your tabs. Show me what you actually do. Click stop when you're done.
  5. Loom opens the video in your browser. Click Copy link. That's it. Paste the link in your email to me.
Loom / Mobile

Loom on iPhone or Android

Same shareable link, recorded from your phone. Good for the questions you can answer on a walk.
  1. Download Loom from the App Store or Play Store. Sign in with the same account if you've already set up desktop.
  2. Tap the red record button. Pick Cam Only for talking head, or Screen if you want to show me something on your phone.
  3. Talk. Tap stop when you're done.
  4. Loom uploads and gives you a shareable link. Tap Copy link and paste it in your email to me.
Fallback / No Loom

Voice memo on your phone

If Loom isn't happening today. Use the recorder app you already have.
  1. iPhone: Open Voice Memos (white icon, red soundwave). Tap the red circle to record. Tap the three dots when you're done, then Share, then email or AirDrop the file.
  2. Android: Open Recorder (Pixel), Voice Recorder (Samsung), or grab Google Recorder from the Play Store for free. Hit the red mic, then share the file when done.
  3. Name the file something like Shallow End - [Your Name] so I know what I'm opening.
  4. If the file is too big for email, drop it in a Google Drive folder, set sharing to "anyone with the link can view", and send me the link.
Recording tips Find a quiet-ish room and don't worry about ums, ahs, or rambles. The longer and messier you talk, the more raw material I get to work with before we sit down. If you fumble a sentence, just restate it and keep rolling. I edit nothing for myself, I just listen.
