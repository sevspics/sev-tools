Ten questions, ten headlines, fifty key points. One pass through this and the series is ready to film.

This is the template from the Loom, built out. Every video keeps your original question and goal, gains a straight-to-the-point headline, and gets five key point beats mapped across the three minutes so nobody rambles.

10 videos · ~3:00 each · 9 referrer-facing + 1 investor · Prepared July 2026
STEP 01

Lock the headline

Each headline is 3 to 5 words, written as the question a referrer is already asking. Click any headline to edit it directly on the card, then mark it locked.

STEP 02

Verify the beats

Tick each key point once the presenter has confirmed it is clinically accurate, or reworded it. Rewording goes in the notes box on the card.

STEP 03

Clear compliance, film

Some cards carry an amber compliance note. Once all three sign-offs on a card are green, it marks itself ready to film and the counter up top moves.

Headline rules

3 to 5 words. Straight to the point. No hookbait, no misleading titles. If the viewer would say "yes, that is exactly my question", it works.

Why beats, not scripts

Three minutes is a long time. Five beats at roughly 35 seconds each keeps the answer moving. The presenter speaks freely inside each beat, the structure does the discipline.

Read this first. The key points below are drafted structure built from the goals in your document. They are a starting shape, not clinical fact. You and the presenters are the experts here, so every point needs your eyes before filming. Anything marked verify is a deliberate prompt for you to confirm or correct.

Every tick, edit and note saves automatically to the cloud, so you can close the tab, switch devices, and Sev sees the same version you do. The copy button builds a plain text status of all ten videos, ready to paste into WhatsApp or email back to Sev.
