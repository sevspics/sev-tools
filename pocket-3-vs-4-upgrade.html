Pocket 3 vs Pocket 4

It looks the same.
But is it worth the upgrade?

The DJI Osmo Pocket 4 lands on shelves looking nearly identical to its predecessor. Same body shape, same 1-inch sensor, same f/2.0 lens. But six things have changed underneath, and one of them might be the reason you finally stop reaching for SD cards.
