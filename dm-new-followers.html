The Organic Play

Message your new followers.

If you're paying for ads, this is the highest-ROI move you're not making. Five minutes a day. No tools. No automations. Just real conversations.

Cost $0

The Insight

Ads bring them in.
DMs convert them.

People scroll Instagram for entertainment. They follow stuff that might be useful, then forget about it 30 seconds later. The DM is the bridge between "interesting" and "buying."

Why Most People Get This Wrong

Automation kills the conversation.

Every guru tells you to set up a ManyChat auto-DM. If you got a message like that, would you actually reply?

The Guru Move

ManyChat auto-DM

"Hey 👋 thanks for the follow! Check out my free guide here 👉 link.bio/freebie"

Generic. Smells like a bot. Killed before it starts.

VS

What Actually Works

A real, personal DM

"Hey, saw you followed... what brought you over? Just here to browse or is there something specific you're working through?"

Curious. Human. Opens a door.

The Playbook

The 3-part DM formula.

STEP 01

Acknowledge the follow

STEP 02

Ask why they're here

STEP 03

Drop something personal

Sample DM · copy / adapt

"Hey [name]! Appreciate the follow 🙌 Quick one, what brought you over? Are you just here for more content or is there a specific thing you're trying to figure out? Also, noticed [you run a café in your area / just had your second kid / are training for a half-marathon]. That's awesome."

Why this works: You're not selling. You're being curious. Their reply tells you exactly what they need, and whether you can help them get it. That's your opening.

When

Send within 24 hours of the follow. After that the heat is gone.

No pitch

Zero CTAs in message #1. Curiosity only. Sell nothing.

Look first

10 seconds on their profile = the "personal" line writes itself.

Reply ≠ pitch

When they reply, ask another question. Don't go for the sale yet.
