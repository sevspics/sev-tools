Campaign rules

Slogan embargo

"More Show For Your Dough" cannot appear anywhere before the media campaign goes live on 31 Aug. Video 1 ships clean. Video 2 onward can land it.

Free stuff only

Value framing leans on free experiences (per their Painted Dog research). Do not sell rides, showbags or food as the draw. They are extras, not the ticket's value.

Roughly 40 to 50k government-subsidised earlybird tickets. Phase 1 at 50% off, Phase 2 at 40% once Phase 1 sells out. Code via database signup. Launch pends a minister's announcement, expected mid Aug.

Organic window

No paid media until the OMD/Marketforce campaign starts late Aug. Your videos ARE the earlybird push. That is the leverage in this deal.

CTA source

Interim CTA: grab half-price tickets now, link in bio. Official lines land in Phoebe's key messages doc. Swap them in the moment it arrives.
